About Us

Our Story

North Star Bakery started at a farmers market in 2009 and we opened our shop on Maple Street in 2012. We've been baking for the neighborhood ever since.

Where our ingredients come from

We use local flour, eggs, and butter whenever we can and we don't use preservatives.
